FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly does LinkOps monitor?

LinkOps checks whether your backlink is still present and monitors attributes such as its target URL, anchor text, rel attributes, source HTTP status and relevant redirects.

Is this an alternative to Ahrefs or Semrush?

Not exactly. Those platforms are primarily SEO intelligence databases. LinkOps is designed around operational monitoring of backlinks you already know about and the campaigns that generated them.

Can I import my existing backlinks?

Yes. You can upload backlinks via CSV, including exports from common SEO workflows, and start monitoring them without rebuilding your backlink inventory manually.

Can LinkOps tell me when a backlink becomes nofollow?

Yes. LinkOps monitors link attributes and can flag changes such as dofollow to nofollow, sponsored or UGC.

What is “Value at Risk”?

It is the recorded acquisition cost associated with backlinks that currently require attention. It does not represent guaranteed SEO or financial loss.

Can agencies manage multiple clients?

Yes. Agency plans are designed around multiple clients, projects, campaigns and team members.
